The Scottish Bagpipe collection keeps the full 35-track Apple Music catalogue. Some tracks link to same-performer recordings on SoundCloud from another release; these are identified as partial playback matches. The full album is linked on Apple Music. The five same-performer SoundCloud selections come from The Best of Military Bands: The King’s Own Scottish Borderers Military Band; matching titles and near-identical durations do not confirm identical mastering. The recording notice is ℗ 2009 Warner Music Group – X5 Music Group; artwork rights remain with their respective owners.

Playback providers

Playback uses SoundCloud’s official widget and Widget API, or Bandcamp’s official embedded player, after the connection notice. Provider branding and source links stay in the players. Playback availability and access conditions depend on the provider and browser. No audio files or stream URLs are bundled with this repository; the virtual Import CD flow saves catalogue entries, not music files.

Bandcamp’s own controls operate its player; Music does not receive its live playback state. The separate desktop-preview connection test uses Flickermood by Forss, the SoundCloud documentation example, with this project’s original disc icon.
